**Read this first.** Every other runbook in `docs/` describes either the GP finder (`production_runbook.md`, NERSC/GreatLakes finder runs) or a superseded statistics path (`architecture.md`'s "Statistics" box, `paper_figures.md`, `CDDF_analysis/hbi/REPRODUCE_HEADLINE.md` = the feed-forward headline). The Paper-1 measurement of record is the **hierarchical (HBI) posterior on the certified corrected-g pack**, plus the **separate BH high-z arm**; this document is the one end-to-end list for it. Written during the Paper-1 code review (2026-08-26); the science content is fixed by PI rulings recorded in the private notes repo.

## 0. Two kinds of reproduction
| mode | what it means | cost | when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Paper regeneration** | rebuild every paper-facing figure, data file and provenance manifest from the FROZEN artifacts (no inference) | minutes | always safe; do it from ONE `paper_figures` commit in ONE pass |
| **B. Full reproduction** | rebuild the frozen artifacts themselves: packs → validation → posterior battery → pooling → BH product | ≈ 15 core-h (CP-2 3×11 min; CP-3 8 base×11 min + 4 deep×19 min; BH 30 s) | only to re-certify; the outputs must reproduce the manifest hashes (products 1, 2, 4 bitwise; product 3 to the sampler's platform determinism) |
The frozen artifacts are the authority; a full reproduction that does not reproduce them is a finding, not a replacement.

Of record: included 21d, 22, 24, 25, 27, 28d (1000 draws each → 6000); excluded 23d (R̂ 1.32) and 26d (R̂ 12.1, the mirror mode) plus the four base runs replaced by their deep reruns. **Reproducibility wording, stated precisely.** (i) A recorded *bitwise* reproduction exists for ONE chain of the *superseded* ckpt-10.10 battery (`real_pack_v1` s22, 2026-08-18 t1 diagnostic) — not for a member of the frozen v2 battery. (ii) **A clean single-member reproduction of the included frozen v2 chain s22 (same pack, seed 20260822, configuration and environment; SLURM 58776700, 2026-08-26, `code_review_20260826/cp3_repro/`) reproduces the frozen run BIT-FOR-BIT: the draws are identical (max|Δ| = 0), the threshold quantiles and the diagnostics block are identical.** The frozen v2 members are seeded (`jax.random.PRNGKey(seed)`, `chain_method="sequential"`) and this demonstrates platform determinism on the current code and environment for one member. (iii) The full six-member pooled production from a clean rerun has NOT been independently demonstrated bit-for-bit; only CP-2 (the mock validation) carries a demonstrated bit-reproduction (`bitrepro_check`, max|Δ| = 0).

## 9. Known limitations of the record (disclosed, not hidden)
- Product 3 has no bit-reproduction demonstration (CP-2 has); the frozen pooled artifact's `inputs` block keys are the run JSON paths while its values are the `_fdraws.npz` hashes (`inputs_note` explains this in artifacts produced after 2026-08-26; the frozen artifact is unchanged).
- The frozen real pack's `.contract_guards.json` records `G_A_partition: FAIL` — the truth-point partition is undefined on a real pack (all-zero truth sentinel); the guard of record is `G_A_real_mode` inside every real run (PASS). `contract_guards_check` now reports NOT_APPLICABLE_REAL_PACK.
- The CP-1 sbatch of record was executed in two jobs at two commits (documentation-only diff); recorded in the committed copy's header.
- `docs/REALDATA_ENV_PRESCRIPTION.md` item 5 ("G-A hard") predates the real-mode substitute; see its 2026-08-26 addendum.
- Everything frozen is on scratch; the manifest is the durable record.
